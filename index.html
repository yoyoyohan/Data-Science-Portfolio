Model evaluation

LLM calibration audit

A study of whether a language model’s stated confidence stays in line with its accuracy as questions get harder.

With Akul Bajpai, I locked the protocol before looking at scores. Five hundred forty MMLU questions, difficulty tiers fixed in advance, five trials each, three models: Claude Haiku, GPT-4o-mini, and Gemini Flash-Lite. Every answer had to come with a confidence from 0 to 100. The analysis asks a standard evaluation question — does verbal confidence track correctness, and does the gap widen on hard items? Claude stayed close to calibrated. The other two grew more overconfident as difficulty rose. We wrote it up as a research paper for the NSRI Summer Research Hackathon 2026, where it placed first in the research category and the team placed 14th overall.

  • Verbal confidence
  • Difficulty tiers
  • 540 MMLU items
  • Three API models

02

Statistical modeling

Is it alpha or just exposure?

An asset-pricing project that separates a stock’s leftover return from its exposure to the market, size, value, and momentum.

Daily excess returns are fit with three nested models — CAPM, the Fama–French three-factor model, and Carhart’s four-factor model — using Ken French’s factor files. Standard errors are Newey–West, because daily residuals are not independent. Nesting the models is the point: if the intercept shrinks once size, value, and momentum enter, the apparent edge was exposure. On 2018 through mid-2026, Apple’s annualized leftover is about 10.5% and the interval includes zero. SPY, the check, has a market beta near 1 and an R² of 0.99.

  • CAPM · FF3 · Carhart
  • Newey–West errors
  • Ken French factors
  • Daily excess returns

Computer vision

Handwritten to Data

A document pipeline that finds the regions on a mixed handwritten page and transcribes each one with a model suited to that region.

Built for Ukrainian pages in a Kaggle competition. YOLOv8x detects seven kinds of region. Handwriting and annotations go to a fine-tuned TrOCR, printed text to Tesseract, formulas to pix2tex, and tables to a table model. Page-level character error is half the scoring rule, so the recognizer carries more weight than the detector. A general vision-language model was a useful baseline. The specialized route is the system I submitted, scoring 0.83157 and finishing 30th of 199+.

  • Layout detection
  • YOLOv8x + TrOCR
  • Seven region types
  • Handwriting and print

Computer vision

Football clip stats

A tracking pipeline that turns a short broadcast clip into possession, passes, shots, shots on target, and goals.

YOLOv8 and ByteTrack follow the players, the ball, and the posts. Jersey colors assign the two teams. A homography maps the frame onto a 105 by 68 meter pitch, and the stat engine reads events off that geometry rather than off the raw pixels. It is written for clips of about ten to thirty seconds. Ball detection, similar kits, and shots with no goal in frame are the limits, and they are documented in the repository.

  • Detection and tracking
  • Pitch homography
  • Event geometry
  • Short broadcast clips

Sports analytics

NJ high school ratings

A ratings site for New Jersey high school sports, built on the spreadsheets coaches already maintain.

The shared Drive folder stays the source of truth, and the app only reads it. Copies are imported, rankings and rating history are rebuilt, and about 600,000 games are served from Postgres. GitHub Actions refreshes the database four times a day. The site is a small FastAPI app on Render, with Supabase holding the database, so a coach can open one URL instead of a workbook.

  • Rankings and results
  • Read-only spreadsheet sync
  • About 600,000 games
  • Scheduled refresh

Sports analytics

Ranking Studio

The publishing desk beside the ratings: a table of schools goes in, a poster comes out.

Names are matched against a library of New Jersey high school crests, so a ranking does not have to be laid out by hand. Type, color, and spacing live as design tokens, which means the poster can be restyled without rewriting the template. The export is a PNG. Built in React and TypeScript as the graphics half of the same sports project.

  • Table to poster
  • School crest matching
  • Design tokens
  • PNG export
