NSRI Summer Research Hackathon 2026 · with Akul Bajpai

LLM calibration audit

Do large language models get more overconfident as the question gets harder?

We locked a protocol before looking at scores: 540 MMLU questions across three subjects and three difficulty tiers, five trials each, temperature 0.3. Claude Haiku, GPT-4o-mini, and Gemini Flash-Lite had to answer and state a confidence from 0 to 100. All 8,100 responses parsed.

Claude was close to calibrated and slightly underconfident (accuracy 0.895, ECE 0.033). GPT-4o-mini and Gemini were overconfident, and the gap grew from easy items to hard ones. For GPT-4o-mini that gap moved from 0.028 to 0.354. Verbal confidence is a weak reliability signal on the hard questions.

  • 14th overall · 110+ teams

02

Kaggle · Handwritten to Data

Ukrainian documents into text

A layout detector and a specialist recognizer for mixed handwritten pages. Finished 30th of 199+, score 0.83157.

Half the competition score is page-level character error, so transcription quality dominates. The pipeline finds regions with YOLOv8x, then routes them: fine-tuned TrOCR for handwriting and annotations, Tesseract for print, pix2tex for formulas, and a table model for cells. A zero-shot vision-language baseline landed near 0.64. The specialized route is what moved the score.

  • 30th · 0.83157
  • YOLOv8x + TrOCR
  • 7 region types
  • 17 submissions

03

Shipped product · FastAPI, Supabase, Render

NJ high school ratings

Rankings, results, and rating history for New Jersey high school sports, refreshed from the spreadsheets coaches already keep.

The shared Drive folder stays the source of truth. The app only downloads copies, rebuilds the ratings, and serves them from Postgres — about 600,000 games. GitHub Actions refreshes the database four times a day. A coach opens one URL. The first load on the free host can take a moment while the service wakes.

  • Live site
  • ~600,000 games
  • Read-only Drive sync
  • 4× daily refresh

React · TypeScript · the publishing desk for the ratings

Ranking Studio

Paste a rankings table. Match each school to its crest. Export a poster.

Ranking Studio is the graphics tool beside the ratings site. School names are matched against a New Jersey high school logo library. Type, color, and spacing live as design tokens, so a poster can be restyled without rewriting the layout. The export is a PNG.

05

Computer vision · YOLOv8 + ByteTrack

Football clip stats

A short broadcast clip becomes possession, passes, shots, shots on target, and goals.

The detector tracks players, the ball, and the posts. Jersey colors split the teams. A pitch homography turns pixels into meters on a 105 by 68 field, and the stat engine reads events off that geometry. It is built for clips of ten to thirty seconds. Ball detection, similar kits, and shots with no goal in frame are the limits, and they are written down in the repo.

  • Possession · passes · shots
  • Pitch in meters
  • Interactive calibration
  • Short clips

06

Empirical asset pricing · 2018 to mid-2026

Is it alpha or just exposure?

When a stock beats the market, how much is unexplained leftover, and how much is size, value, and momentum?

Daily excess returns are fit with three nested models — CAPM, Fama–French three-factor, and Carhart four-factor — using Ken French’s factors and Newey–West standard errors. Apple’s annualized leftover is about 10.5% and barely shrinks as factors are added. The interval includes zero (t = 1.53). SPY, the check, has a market beta near 1, alpha on zero, and an R² of 0.99. A point estimate that fails the test is the result.

  • AAPL α ≈ 10.5% · p = 0.13
  • SPY R² = 0.99
  • Newey–West errors
  • Nested factor models
