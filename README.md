# Yohan Deshpande

Data science student. Projects in applied statistics, machine learning, and sports analytics.

**Live page:** https://yoyoyohan.github.io/Data-Science-Portfolio/

The work starts from a raw source, builds a measurement, and checks whether that measurement means what it claims. Six repositories, four kinds of work.

## Model evaluation

Whether a language model’s stated confidence matches its accuracy.

**[LLM calibration audit](https://github.com/yoyoyohan/llm-calibration-audit)** — with Akul Bajpai. A locked protocol on 540 MMLU items, difficulty tiers fixed in advance, three API models, each answer paired with a verbal confidence. Research paper for the NSRI Summer Research Hackathon 2026: first in the research category, 14th overall.

## Statistical modeling

How much of a return is factor exposure, and what is left over.

**[Is it alpha or just exposure?](https://github.com/yoyoyohan/alpha-or-exposure)** — CAPM, Fama–French, and Carhart on daily excess returns, with Newey–West standard errors. Nesting the models shows whether a leftover return shrinks once size, value, and momentum are included.

## Computer vision

Handwritten pages and broadcast clips turned into structured data.

**[Handwritten to Data](https://github.com/yoyoyohan/handwritten-to-data)** — YOLOv8x finds seven region types on Ukrainian pages; TrOCR, Tesseract, pix2tex, and a table model transcribe them. Score 0.83157, 30th of 199+.

**[Football clip stats](https://github.com/yoyoyohan/football-clip-stats)** — YOLOv8, ByteTrack, jersey colors, and a pitch homography turn a short broadcast clip into possession, passes, shots, and goals.

## Sports analytics

Ratings from the spreadsheets coaches already keep, and the graphics that publish them.

**[NJ high school ratings](https://github.com/yoyoyohan/sportsbusinessrankings)** — read-only sync from Drive, about 600,000 games in Postgres, refreshed four times a day. [Open the site](https://sportsbusinessrankings.onrender.com).

**[Ranking Studio](https://github.com/yoyoyohan/designtool)** — match a rankings table to New Jersey high school crests and export a poster.
